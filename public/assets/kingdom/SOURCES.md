# Selected environment assets

KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 FREE by Kay Lousberg (CC0). Original package license is retained here. Only the 24 used glTF models and their referenced buffers/textures are extracted.

Blue/red castles, towers, homes, wells and flags; straight wall/gate, wooden fence; tree_single_A/B, rock_single_A/C, hill_single_A/C, mountain_A_grass; tent, barrel, crate_A_small and sack. Seven additional models come from Kenney Nature Kit and one cart from Kenney Fantasy Town Kit, each with its retained license in the respective asset directory. Static decoration shares geometry/materials through InstancedMesh; animated base castles retain independent roots for damage shake. These props do not change combat boundaries or targeting.
