# Selected environment assets

KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 FREE by Kay Lousberg (CC0). Original package license is retained here. Only ten used glTF models and their referenced buffers/textures were extracted.

Blue/red building_castle and building_tower_A; wall_straight and wall_straight_gate; tree_single_A/B; rock_single_A/C. Tree and rock geometry/materials are shared through InstancedMesh. These are decorative and do not change combat boundaries or targeting.
